Market snapshot

Typical value is the Zillow Home Value Index (ZHVI) — a smoothed, mix-adjusted estimate of the value of the whole housing stock in the ZIP, not the price of the homes that actually sold. It is not a median sale price and does not come from the MLS. Level and year-over-year change are read from the same series at the same vintage, and each tile carries its own as-of date because Zillow’s ZIP vintages are not aligned. Both core ZIPs are down mid-single-digits year-over-year, in line with the whole Eastside — a broad softening that favors buyers and renters who can wait.

Takeaway: All four target catchments carry a 9/10 GreatSchools rating — a clear step up from the reference home's Ardmore zone (4/10). Somerset posts the strongest scores but the priciest, hilliest homes; Audubon's catchment is tiny (frequently 0–1 listings) and nearest Microsoft; Newport Heights and Woodridge give the most central Bellevue commutes. Use the school filter below to compare homes by catchment, and always confirm a specific address's exact assignment with the district boundary tool. The map and table also surface catchments outside this table — Other catchment covers Bellevue elementaries whose ratings have not been hand-verified here, so treat those as unrated rather than as equivalent to the four targets. Only Ardmore and Lake Hills are labelled Weak school, because those are the two low ratings that were checked against the primary source.

Source: school ratings & test scores are taken from each school's GreatSchools profile (greatschools.org), verified June 2026 — math & reading (ELA) from the 2024-25 WA OSPI assessments; Lake Hills verified Aug 2026 (English 38%, math 40%, vs WA state averages of 53% and 43%). These figures update yearly and are not changed by the automated refresh — re-check the current profile before relying on them.

Prefer the R chip when you want the full price history: realtor.com keeps the withdraw-and-relist events that Redfin and Zillow drop, so it shows what a home has really been asking and for how long. A grey ↓ total badge in the Price column reports the drop since the current campaign began, which is often far larger than the most recent cut alone.

Get the weekly digest by email

One email on Saturday, and only when something actually happened — a new listing, a price cut, a home going pending or leaving the market, or a real move in the mortgage rate. A quiet week sends nothing. It carries the same “what changed” list and the same read you see on this page, with links straight to each home.

Found something wrong? Tell me.

This tracker is built in the open and the data is scraped, so mistakes get through — a stale price, a home that sold weeks ago, a school catchment that doesn't match what the district says for your street. Corrections are the most useful thing you can send.
